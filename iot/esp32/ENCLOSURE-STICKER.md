## What the light means

**When you switch it on**

| Light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue | Starting up. Wait about 20 seconds |
| Purple | Press BOOT now to change the WiFi. 4 seconds only |
| Red | Wrong WiFi password, or no network found |
| Orange | On WiFi, but it cannot reach the server |
| Green | Ready. Nothing to do |

**While it is working**

| Light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | Everything is normal |
| Green with a blue blink | Not reaching the dashboard. Nothing is lost |
| Bright blue | The room is too cold |
| Yellow | Getting hot, humid or smoky |
| Orange | Humidity is too high |
| Red | DANGER — heat or smoke. Check the room |

## What the sound means

- One long continuous tone — SMOKE. Act now.
- Fast beeping — too hot, or too humid.
- Slow beeping — warning.

## What is plugged in

| CH | Smoke sensor | Aircon remote |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fitted | Fitted |
| 2 | Fitted | Fitted |
| 3 | Empty | Empty |
| 4 | Empty | Empty |

CH3 and CH4 are spare. A technician can fit them — ask ICTU.
The dashboard uses these same CH numbers.

## To change the WiFi

1. Plug it in. Do not press anything yet.
2. Wait about 4 seconds, until the light turns purple.
3. **While it is purple**, press the small BOOT button once.
4. On your phone, join the WiFi **CSPC-ICTU-Sensor** (no password). A setup page opens by
   itself — pick a 2.4 GHz network.

> ⚠️ **Never hold the BOOT button while plugging it in.** It will stop working. Unplug and
> start again.

## Never

- Open it or touch the wiring while it is plugged in.
- Take out the small round battery inside.
- Connect it to a 5 GHz WiFi network.

**Do not open the box.** Anything not on this sticker — contact ICTU or the development team.
